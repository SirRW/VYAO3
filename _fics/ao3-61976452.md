## Chapter 1

#### Work Notes

For JayVik week Day 1: beginning/end 💙

### Text

Recently, Jayce has been imagining if their partnership could ever come to an end.

*This* is still brand new, only a couple of months since Viktor interrupted his morbid solitude on the rooftop that day, even less since they've had their own lab and started being together almost 24/7. And it isn't in Jayce's nature to get this anxious.

Maybe because he's used to working alone, maybe because no one's ever made their way do quickly into his life, and certainly not ever this close, this overwhelmingly intricate.

Maybe because Caitlyn, the little Minx, just had to tell him to *enjoy the honeymoon phase* *while it lasts* when Jayce told her about Viktor and how they're practically glued to each other now. He's not even sure what's that supposed to mean, or why partners would have a honeymoon phase. But it keeps bugging him.

"You're being ridiculous." Viktor assures him, eyes still on the blackboard. But Jayce isn't really sure if Viktor thinks the idea itself is crazy or if it isn't that important to begin with. He's not even paying attention.

Jayce walks around their lab, watching everything they're building together, giddy just by imagining how it'll look a few months from now, a few *years*. He frowns and stubbornly approaches Viktor again.

"Well, it could happen."

"Jayce." Viktor keeps taking notes, like his brain isn't even phased by having to converse and write the formulas at the same time. "Stop worrying about it."

So he does, because he can't really argue with Viktor and because the work keeps him occupied. For now.

⚙

"I dreamed I was exposed as a fraud." It happened last night, as soon as his head hit the pillow he started going over the day and his brain probably got full of thoughts of catastrophic scenarios.

"A fraud?" Viktor says, looking at him through the spectacles. Jayce thinks it's nice when he wears them.

"Yeah. Someone tipped Heimerdinger or something." Jayce tries to remember but his dreams don't exactly have the most cohesive plotlines. Maybe nobody even said anything but the counselor somehow just knew? "Turns out my degree was a fake, I conned my way through the whole academy..."

Viktor stares at him in mild amusement, sipping his coffee. He's been drinking an alarming amount of it since they basically refuse to leave the lab nowadays.

"So? Did you?"

Jayce frowns.

"You got mad at me." It's fuzzy but Jayce remembers the feeling, Viktor's eyes, the disappointment.

"Over your fake degree? Oh, please." Viktor's expression does something strange for a second but it changes before Jayce can think too much about it. "Any other nightmares we should be concerned about?"

"No not really, but, Viktor, listen-"

"Jayce, last week I dreamt I couldn't tie my shoes. Should I be afraid of that?"

"Maybe!"

Viktor snorts. "I'd just make you do it for me, then. Come on, back to work."

⚒

Viktor's changing clothes. It's not uncommon, they practically live here, so Jayce merely faces the other way and does his best to keep busy. He never leaves, though, none of them do. They've become accustomed to this routine, this way of working together and keeping their privacies at the same time somehow. It's comfortable.

He finishes up what he's doing and just keeps staring are the work, the notes, hearing the sound of Viktor's clothes ruffling, the metal of his braces against the buttons.

"What about the investors...?"

"What about them?" Viktor asks and Jayce bites his lip. "Jayce?"

"What if we disagree on the politics, I mean"

"Then we'll have a discussion about it"

"What if we *really* disagree"

"We'll talk about it. And you'll come to agree with me."

"What if I don't?"

"Then you've chosen death." Viktor clearly aims for funny but it's so *not funny* when his delivery is this dry. Jayce doesn't even know why he smiles, come *on*, why does he? "We'll use our fists, sadly there's no other way around it, we'll fight like real men."

"Viktor, be serious"

"It's the Zaun way"

"It is not" Jayce is rolling his eyes but his laughter almost escapes. Ah, he's so tired, hungry, probably a bit unkempt for his standards even, but still he wants nothing but to stay here and laugh at this man's nonsense.

"'Course it is you wouldn't know, would you?"

"No, I suppose not." He sighs, staring at the sky, at the few stars he can see. "Okay, then I'll take you."

"I can't really run away so I have no choice but end you with my cane, sorry." Jayce hears his cane hit the ground, probably to make a point.

"It's okay, it's a nice death."

"A very noble one"

"Oh yeah, my partner disagreed with me and-" He's not expecting to be touched. Viktor made his way over here while Jayce was distracted, his fingers now curiously touch Jayce's scalp, feather light.

"If I remember correctly *you* disagreed with *me*"

*It's the same thing*, he wants to say, *it's literally the same thing*, but it gets forgotten as Viktor's fingers stay instead of leaving and the sky suddenly feels much closer, much more like heaven.

ིཐཋྀ

Jayce comes close to death when he steals Viktor's pen. While he's using it.

"*What* are you doing." Oh, he sounds pissed.

"Testing a hypothesis."

"Jayce."

"Are you upset?"

"That you stole my pen? Yes, Jayce, I'm a little upset."

Viktor keeps scowling at him, then his eyes go back to the page like he can, somehow, write the rest of his ideas with his mind alone.

"Did you...want to see me upset?" Viktor questions.

"What? No, Viktor. No, just...how long it would last?"

Viktor frowns, confused. Then his face does something funny as he quickly puts two and two together. And he *laughs*. Full blown laugh, hands on his belly laugh and Jayce thinks that maybe this is it, he's lost his marbles, they've officially spent too many nights in this lab.

"Jayce we're not *breaking up* over a pen." Jayce tries to ignore the way Viktor says it like they're-

"I know, V." Jayce runs a hand through his hair. "But I thought I could... extrapolate the results?"

"A very clumsy experiment. Doomed methodology. Cursed results, likely."

"I'm sleep deprived, gimme a break." Jayce chuckles.

"Well, if you're this adamant on it, you know, there's a much better way to ensure we'll stay together."

Viktor kisses him.

Jayce doesn't get the courtesy of a moment to understand it, not even a single second to process the fact Viktor just got impossibly closer, slammed his lips into his, fisted his tie and *demanded* more, Jayce's lips opening obediently to comply.

He feels Viktor's finger's on his hair again, bit they don't relax him like last time, they make him overexcited and make him mimic the gesture, feeling Viktor's soft hair between his fingers, pulling him closer and deepening the kiss, Viktor's voice going whiny for a second untill his partner finally breaks them apart.

"There." Viktor says, completely satisfied with himself apparently.

"Viktor... I...I'm not sure if I get it."

"If one of us is about to leave, how could we go through with it if the other does this?" It's ludicrous. Viktor needs to sleep... probably.

It doesn't even make sense.

There's tons of reasons why they would still want to leave. And it's such an oversimplified solution. Is it safe? *Of course not*, he can practically hear Viktor's voice inside his head.

But Jayce's lips are tingling, his eyes won't stop searching for Viktor's mouth again, addiction coming naturally with a single taste.

So maybe Jayce understands. It would indeed be very *very* hard to resist something like this. Maybe they'll stay together forever, after all.
