## Chapter 1

“We are in accordance then, Councilors. The nation of Zaun shall be granted its independence effective immediately.”

*Oh fuck*, Jayce thinks.

Not because Zaun is a free nation. No, that is wonderful news. Great news. News that makes Viktor’s brassy eyes come to life, like sunlight glinting off of amber jewels. Gold under a forge, burning with excitement.

And Jayce can’t look away. He’s trapped by a topaz tinted gaze, and it’s not even directed at him. Rather at the horizon. Hopeful, the way that he was when they’d first cranked the Hexcore.

Bathed in blue light, Viktor had floated across from him, grinning ear to ear. Alight with mischief. New discovery.

Utterly stunning.

*Fuck.*

Jayce tears his eyes away from his partner, who is still oblivious to the internal crisis occurring next to him. Jayce claps with the rest of the council, plastering a winning smile on his face. Across the room, he feels another set of golden eyes on his. Mel’s.

She has that look on her face. Like the cat that caught the canary. She gives him a knowing smile, and a glance at Viktor so quick Jayce could’ve imagined it. Then she turns back to the others.

It reminds him of when they first met. Viktor lying swiftly about trying to sneak them into his bedroom and Jayce floundering in complete panic. Mel took one look at them, and she knew. She always does.

Jayce supposes he’s never been good at subtlety.

Breathing a sigh of relief, he steals himself before glancing back to Viktor, who is looking right at him. His gaze rips the air from his lungs completely. As though someone has set a hexhammer on his chest and simply left it there.

Zaun is a free nation. The whole world is healing. And Jayce is in agony.

Jayce nearly jumps out of his skin, and Caitlyn groans.

“Vi, you mustn’t sneak up on people like that.”

Her words are scolding, but the affection in Caitlyn’s voice is evident. Even to Jayce, who has the emotional intelligence of a screwdriver.

“That’s okay,” he says, chuckling as the hairs on his neck settle, “even with the cane Viktor still manages to startle me sometimes.”

At the mention of Viktor, Caitlyn and Vi share a look. Jayce’s eyes dart back and forth between them. Caitlyn glances back to Jayce, a smile toying at her mouth.

“I am going to go help my mother out in the garden,” she says, patting him on the shoulder. “I’ll leave you to it.”

Since when had Councilor Kiramman been interested in gardening?

Jayce watches Cait leave, somewhat hoping she’ll turn around and come back. She doesn’t.

“Well?” Vi says, leaning against the doorframe. “We going to chat or what?”

“My lab partner, Viktor…”

Vi nods to that. Her lack of surprise tells Jayce that Caitlyn knew about his hopeless case far before he did.

“He is from the undercity. From Zaun.”

Now Vi’s eyes widen in surprise. She smirks, a lopsided almost canine sort of look.

“The Man of Progress… into one of us. Never thought I’d see the day.”

“I need your assistance,” Jayce says, the words spilling out naturally, like a grant proposal. “I need to understand your customs and culture in this regard. I can’t just go about this like usual.”

This is different. This matters. This is Viktor.

Always curious, hard headed, intelligent Viktor. Viktor, whose honey thick accent laces every word, and sticks in the back of Jayce’s mind, seeping into the crevices of his life.

Viktor has become an irrevocable aspect of his life. He will handle this with the utmost care that he would any of his projects.

“Alright, Piltie,” Vi says, rubbing her hands together. “Class is in session.”

Jayce still nearly shits his pants, but Viktor simply smiles politely at them. Jinx ruffles the kids hair, blue at the tips.

“This…” she says, grinning somewhat maniacally, “is my *protege*.”

Viktor nods along in agreement and asks what they have made. Jayce fears that he is going to have to workshop another public explosion apology.

“Go on, show ‘em, Isha.” Jinx says.

She nods and clicks a button. The contraption sputters and clanks before spitting out a puff of glitter and…

A beetle.

A very sparkly, very pink beetle.

“Well done,” Viktor says, and Jayce looks at him in blank confusion.

“This one’s Stinkmaw,” Jinx says, and Isha grabs the beetle, running off to grab a replacement. “The current reigning champion.”

She comes back with another bug and pops it in the machine, excitedly clicking the button again. This one comes out blue.

“How creative,” Viktor says, not even flinching as Isha grabs his hand and puts the bug into his open palm. “It can even adjust the coloring.”

The girls gleam with pride, and Viktor extends the bug towards Jayce.

“Would you like to-”

Viktor assesses the look on Jayce’s face and hands the bug back to Jinx.

“You are both very talented,” he says, “I look forward to seeing your future endeavors.”

Isha gleams with pride and Jinx gives a mock salute as they walk away.

“So uh-”

“Beetle battles,” Viktor answers before Jayce can even ask. “Children’s pastime here in Zaun.”

He smiles, clearly nostalgic.

“What was the name of your beetle?”

“Ah, I did not participate,” Viktor says, “I would always feel guilty and let them go.”

Jayce chuckles at the thought of a small Viktor tearfully releasing his insect friends, freeing them from the heat of battle.

He jumps when he feels a warm hand in his, and Viktor is tugging him along towards the next booth.

Minutes turn into hours, and they bounce from person to person and invention to invention. Jayce marvels at the relaxed slope of Viktor’s shoulders and the ease of his words as he exchanges botany notes with Ekko.

He basks in the way that Viktor laughs, throwing his head back and his eyebrows furrowed. He notices how Viktor rolls his eyes fondly when Jayce wrinkles his nose at the local food. He does try it, and acquiesces that it is actually rather good.

He watches intently as Viktor lightly sways to the music, leaning subtly against Jayce’s palm on his back. When Heimerdinger takes over the banjo, they make eye contact, barely holding in their laughter.

And then it turns out that Heimerdinger can *seriously* play a banjo.

By the time that they begin the walk back the moon is setting, and the stars are fading with the close approach of dawn.

Viktor’s whole face is relaxed, and the edges of a smile rest on his lips. Jayce can’t tear his eyes away.

The electricity in the air remains, even after all their goodbyes were said and everyone packed up and went their separate ways again. Though many with a few new blueprints.

“Thank you,” Viktor says.

Jayce looks at him, eyes wide.

“I will never forget this night.”

“I hope not, it’s supposed to be an annual event.”

Viktor laughs.

They arrive at the apartment, standing just outside his door atop the white and gold splatter. Jayce’s shoe dips just slightly into the crater.

“There is just one thing that I do not understand,” Viktor says, turning so they are face to face. “Why you would put all of that together, an annual event… for me?”

Jayce freezes, eyes wide and stuttering as he tries to find the right words. There are simply none. There are no words to accurately describe the way his heart leaps in his chest whenever he sees Viktor so much as breathe. To describe how, if Viktor so much as asked, he’d give him anything from his own beating heart to the sun itself.

“I have a hypothesis. Might I test it?” Viktor asks.

Jayce nods and the next thing he knows, there is a warm pair of lips on his own. Soft, and slightly chapped, and before he can even process they are gone.

He opens his eyes and sees Viktor, looking at him with an eyebrow raised. He opens his mouth to speak, and Jayce pulls him in by the collar.

He kisses Viktor with fervor, with worship, moving his lips slow and gentle. With a care that he has never taken before. He soaks in the feeling, the warmth and the tingling of the hand running through his hair.

It’s only when his lungs begin to squeeze that he pulls back, burying his face in Viktor’s neck. He feels Viktor’s chest rise as he laughs, the sound low and sultry.

“When did you know?” Jayce asks, not daring to look up for how red his cheeks are.

“I had my suspicions from the day we met,” Viktor replies, laying his soft fingers across the top of Jayce’s head, brushing past his burning ears. “My theory became far more concrete when you took me to lunch and then furthermore when you bought me flowers. Even if they did explode.”

Jayce groans, glaring down at the painted ground beneath them.

“Now, of course, I have been proven correct.”

The sun shines in through Jayce’s window and he groans, pressing a pillow over his head. Said pillow is then promptly removed.

“Viktor,” Jayce whines.

His complaining is silenced by a kiss, and suddenly being awake isn’t quite so bad. Viktor pulls back, brown hair messy and his eyes alight.

“It is nearly ten. There are only so many hours in the day.”

Jayce rolls his eyes, grabbing Viktor by the waist and flopping over, propping himself above him. Jayce takes a moment to let his eyes roam the expanse of milky skin before kissing him lazily.

Jayce presses a kiss to the mole by his eye, then the one by his mouth. He kisses the three on his neck, heat flooding over him as Viktor hums from above him.

“I must ask,” Viktor says, “as I did not get the chance to last night.”

“Mhm,” Jayce replies, lying down on the bed next to him and resting his head on Viktor’s chest. He runs a hand through Viktor’s hair, reveling in the way he leans into the touch.

“Was there any other reason why you were acting so… *odd* these past few weeks?”

Viktor’s gaze bores into his own and Jayce ekes out an apologetic smile.

“That is a rather long story.”

Jayce finds himself once again sitting on an overly plush loveseat. Caitlyn, along with Vi wearing an incredibly smug face, sitting across from him. Only this time Viktor is seated next to him, warm against his side.

“I told you I’m a genius,” Vi says, and Cait rolls her eyes as she pours another cup of tea. “You’d better save a spot in that lab for me.”

“I cannot believe I took your advice,” Jayce grumbles, pulling a hand down his face. He can feel Viktor silently laughing next to him, his face stone cold but his shoulders shaking.

“Clearly it worked,” Vi says, gesturing to them. “I’m telling you cupcake. You take street smart and book smart, and you’ve got two whole smarts.”

Caitlyn laughs, leaning against her shoulder, and Jayce sighs. Vi continues to ramble about the self-help book she’s going to write. Caitlyn does nothing to stop this. In fact she offers to fund it. Jayce fears for the state of the new world. Viktor though is quiet, pensive.

He speaks, finally.

“The smarts would cancel out.”
