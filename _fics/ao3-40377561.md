### Text

“Viktor?” Jayce mutters, cracking open the door to the lab. After weeks, he finally has a night free of the council. He wants to spend it with Viktor, he needs to.

Jayce couldn’t believe he’d had to waste what little time Viktor had left attending council meetings instead. He supposed maybe if he tried hard enough to be without him, it would make it easier when he’d be gone for good. Jayce knew he couldn’t let himself get comfortable anymore, knowing Viktor would be taken from him, couldn’t get attached only to lose his better half, and then what would he do? It would be worse that way, they were both better off. He can’t indulge.

But he shakes the thought from his head. *Don’t think about that now.* He ignores the knot in his stomach and opens the door wider. There’s no response, and his chest tightens. The fear that he’d once again be stumbling upon Viktor’s limp and bloody body takes hold. The thought occurs to him that maybe he’s too late this time. Shakily ,he continues as he steps in “Viktor? Are you there?” He must have sounded at least somewhat panicked because this time, he received a reply.

“Where else would I be?” responds a familiar voice, and the relief the councilor feels is immeasurable. It almost overwhelms the guilt that sparks hearing how *tired* he sounds. Almost.

Jayce finally steps in, trying to ignore the building nerves as he says “home, maybe? I figured you might have been asleep by now.”

“I sleep here, actually.” He says matter-of-factly, bordering on annoyance. “Home doesn’t have what I need. *I* can’t afford to waste time.” An *‘unlike you’* goes unspoken, but the accusatory tone is enough for Jayce’s mind to fill the gaps. The guilt grows more ravenous.

“Viktor that’s not…” he tries to find the right words, tries not to patronize, he doesn’t want to upset Viktor any further. “That's not right, you need rest sometimes- I know working hard on this is important” it’s detrimental. It’s their last hope. “But you’re not gonna get any further with the hexcore If you’re not thinking straight. It can’t help you if you won’t help yourself.”

“Oh you’d know all about helping yourself, wouldn’t you *councilor* ” Viktor can’t help but snap. He’s not upset with Jayce, he’s really not. But he’s overwhelmed, reaching his limits, and Jayce just happens to be in the wrong place at the wrong time. Viktor can’t *help* it.

“This isn’t about that.” Jayce’s brows furrow, he feels himself frown. He forces any outrage down, he refuses to argue with Viktor, not now, *he’s wasted enough time already* “I know I haven’t been around much, but I’m here now,” he sighs, trying not to come across as defensive “I missed you.”

Viktor scoffs at that, and Jayce’s heart drops down to his stomach. The guilt is eating away at him now. He knows what it’s like when Viktor is like this, he knows he’s about to be torn into. He makes no move to stop it.

“I missed you too.” He laughs bitterly “But I don’t think it matters. Missing each other doesn’t change anything,” His voice is growing shaky, with what Jayce can only assume is a concoction of anger and hurt. Every word sinks in one by one, they make his body feel like lead. “I’m always here, and you never are.”

“I have to be, Viktor, I don’t *want* to leave you alone-“ Jayce begins, before Viktor cuts him off with a shout.

“Why is it always about what *you* want?!” He swivels around in his seat, the other man can’t help but flinch. “I want to *live* Jayce, not that it matters to you.” He’s facing Jayce now, glaring, face contorted into a perfect picture of rage. “You clearly don’t care and I’m sure you’d rather be elsewhere, you’ve made your choice.” He pauses, expression growing stern and cold “so I’d suggest you leave and let me get back to *my* research.” Jayce thinks it could kill him. The way the air is knocked out of his chest, the way his heart seems to shake his entire body. His eyes are burning before he can blink away the tears. His next words are quiet, he barely hears himself as they leave his lips

“You think I don’t care…?” He feels like an *idiot.* Naive, oblivious because he just doesn’t understand. Viktor’s words *hurt;* he never thought Viktor would hurt him. In hindsight, he should have expected it. He couldn’t be so terrible and expect no consequences right? Viktor deserved to hurt him, he just didn’t want it to be because he’d done so first.

There’s no response at first, Viktor remains defiantly looking away, focused on the hexcore with his back to Jayce. He can’t look at him, he thinks he’ll break, he’s barely keeping composure. But the cold front begins to crack when Jayce murmurs “I’m sorry.”

At first, he wants to scream, to tell him *fuck* his guilt and *fuck* his apologies, but when he turns around, the sighs makes his stomach sink. Jayce can’t look at him, eyes fixated on the wall behind him, struggling to focus as his mind starts to drift. Viktor’s glare softens, if slightly, at the sight of the other’s eyes, fearful and glazed. “Jayce- That isn’t what I meant-“ he rushes out. *of course* *he didn’t, how could he?* He *knows* it’s his own anxieties, irrational ones, that made him believe so. Jayce just happened to be there at the wrong place and the wrong time, when they finally decided to spill over. It was anger in the heat of a moment, and now that it’s passed, the regret begins to build.

Jayce doesn’t respond, all Viktor can hear is shallow breaths, growing faster, as Jayce struggles to find the words. “N-no, no it’s okay. You don’t have to-to-“ his breath hitches and he squeezes his eyes shut, Viktor’s heart sinks at the sight.

A frustrated yell precedes the harsh scrubbing of brimming tears, leaving red prints against Jayce’s skin. Viktor resists the urge to interrupt as the other sucks in a breath, and continues “You don’t have to l-lie for my sake. I’ve been a terrible partner and I-I can’t expect you to just take that.” He closes his eyes again, and shakes his head. When he opens them, they stare at the floor, pointedly anywhere but Viktor. He fears if he looks at him, he will simply break. “You deserve better. I’m sorry.”

The last words come out shaky, and cracked, accompanied by a quivering lip and tears that Jayce just can’t seem to hold back. Viktor feels pain ebbing in his chest, the previous tightness in his lungs growing tenfold. His vision blurs, and he stands, stepping towards the other. “Jayce-“ he flinches, and Viktor’s mind screams that he needs to *fucking fix this* .

But part of him didn’t want to. Part of him was *hurt* by Jayce’s absence, felt *replaced* , *abandoned* , *unwanted* . Jayce was there, *occasionally* , and it wasn't enough, not like he used to be. Viktor never liked change in his routine, he knew Jayce felt the same. It just wasn’t *easy* for them. And honestly, he’s sure he could have managed.

Or at least, could have had he not received a terminal diagnosis, had he not collapsed while Jayce *wasn’t there.* And when that didn’t change much afterwards, it wasn’t enough. The occasional visits , five minute catch ups, quick exchanges could never be enough. Not when he couldn’t even spend the *last few weeks of his life* with one of the only people he’s ever loved, *cared about more than anything.*

He knew it was reasonable that it hurt, but he also knew retaliation was the last thing he needed now that the guilt was cutting so *deep* . He’d wanted more, but he knows Jayce did too. He knows he hated being away, going to so many events, pretending to be someone he isn’t. He *knows.* Jayce still *tried* to visit, *tried* to catchup, *wanted to be with him.*

Viktor wishes he could have just been *satisfied* , he wishes he didn’t care so much now. The regret is eating him alive. Gods forbid he dies, and Jayce thinks he wasn’t enough. That Viktor thought he didn’t care. He’d never forgive himself, He’d hate himself, and it would be the fault of Viktor’s words.

Only now does he feel the burning trails running down his face. The painful tensing of his throat. He doesn’t know when he started crying, but he won’t let it stop him from trying to clarify. “Jayce, it can’t get better than you. you’re...you're one of the best things have in my life, that I’ve *ever* had.” He swallows a sob, “I wouldn’t want anyone else.”

Jayce seems almost inconsolable, shaking, staring blankly at his fidgeting hands as he holds his breath, and stifles his cries. It takes a moment before he responds. “I don’t get it.” He’s so quiet, so scared. Viktor couldn’t believe he’d done this to him. “Viktor, you don’t have to settle for me. I haven’t done enough, I haven’t proven that I care, I do, I *promise* I do-“ his breaths speed up again, his words break off into a cry, and he slams a hand over his mouth, looking away. He tries to force it down, desperately needing to regain composure but he just *can't,* he needs Viktor to *know* . “I can do better-“

Only now does he look at him, feeling ashamed under his gaze. Soft, beautiful, and golden as it was, Jayce feels pierced by it. He feels *terrified* . But the words leave before he can stop them.

“Viktor, I love you. I love you so much.” He can’t *breathe* “I’m sorry Viktor, I’m sorry.” Panic is sinking its claws deep in his mind, he feels it prickling along his skin. He hates it, he hates himself for allowing this to happen. Viktor deserves better than this, he needs to be strong for him. But here he is, choking on his words before him, after leaving him no less, Jayce is *burdening* him, adding to a list of a million other problems, and he feels so, so, suffocatingly *guilty.* “I’m so sorry.”

Viktor doesn’t know what to say. He knew Jayce was impressionable, too trusting, and he took everything to heart. So why did he say it? Jayce made him feel more loved and cared for than practically anyone else. When Jayce said he loved him, he knew he meant it, he knew the devastation he’d shown at his prognosis was just as real. He couldn’t let Jayce think he felt uncared for. Jayce would never forgive himself. Viktor couldn’t let that happen. The last thing he wanted was for Jayce to feel like he wasn’t enough for him, he wanted Jayce to think of him fondly once he was gone, *he didn’t want to be a regret.*

But Viktor doesn’t know what to do, he’s barely holding it together himself. “I know-I know you do- you’ve shown so much love to me Jayce- I’m sorry for acting like you haven’t” He’s rambling, missing the point “ I love you too Jayce. I’m sorry.” He’s sorry that Jayce had to fall in love with someone who won’t last, someone who will break his heart, leave him alone. Logically he knows it’s not his fault, he doesn’t want to die and he’s so goddamn *scared.* He doesn’t want to die yet, and he doesn’t want Jayce to see him die, and wither away.

He wraps his arms tightly around the other, the two of them practically crying into each other’s shoulders. There’s a moment of silence filled only by quiet cries, before Viktor mutters. “I want to spend the rest of my life with you Jayce.” The admission leaves his lips quietly, and broken. Barely a whisper. It practically stuns Jayce into silence.

“I…” Jayce begins, before tightening his hold on Viktor “I-I’d do anything- I’d do anything to spend that time with you, I love you.” the words come more confidently than they had previously. He knows Viktor’s time is precious, he wants to be there so *badly.* “I never wanted to leave you.”

The solemn tone leaves Viktor with a heavy sense of guilt that he can’t shake. Jayce is so overwhelmingly *distressed* and he can’t help but feel as if it’s his fault. “I know.” he says, pulling away for a moment to wipe his eyes. Jayce watches, and he looks so *tired.* Viktor can’t imagine that he himself looks much better. “I think…I think we’re done, for the night.”

Jayce’s brow furrows before he looks to Viktor’s desk, then back to him. “Oh-oh yeah, we should head home. I don’t think either of us are in a fit state to work right now.” he states, Viktor nodding. The taller man then wipes his face, clearing his throat before continuing “I’ll walk with you.”

Viktor can’t help but smile softly, despite how bone-deep *exhausted* he felt. Sleep sounded good, getting there with Jayce sounded even better, despite the night’s events.

“I’d like that.”
