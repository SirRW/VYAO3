“You have to deal with the consequences of your actions, Viktor,” the Dean of the University had said to him at the time.

If anyone else had to be punished for a transgression, Viktor would probably agree with it, every action has a reaction and every mistake must be corrected. That’s how things work in the real world. But this was different, he wasn’t in the wrong to begin with so why did he have to pay for it? He was just doing his job as a researcher in the area of Applied Physics, what’s the matter if he had a little bit of an accident? That’s inevitable when it comes to doing experiments, sometimes you have to blow up a few things to reach a big discovery. And he was so close!

He couldn’t blame them, Ethics class has always been the biggest bore of all since his own undergrad days, he himself wouldn’t be there giving that lecture if it his position in the PhD program wasn’t at risk. “It is important to take note of these codes because they aren’t just a way to guide you ethically through your active career, but also they are something you can use as a new perspective in the world of physics.”

“Didn’t ya blow up a whole lab, doc?” The blue haired girl, who sits in the front row of his two classes just to throw paper balls at the trash can, joked in a very sardonic tone. “How ethical was that?”
