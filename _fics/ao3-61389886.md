### Text

In retrospect it shouldn’t really be a surprise that Jayce’s self-sufficient and very stubborn partner would try to leave immediately after being brought back from the dead.

Viktor always did what he wanted. It was no surprise that even after death he was the same. Jayce just usually let him do whatever he wanted.

Emphasis on the usually.

“Are you kidding me Viktor?,” it should have been a question but it was said like a statement. Jayce’s eyebrows raised in shock. “Please tell me you’re joking you can’t leave.”

Viktor actually looked taken aback, which was a stark relief from his previous emotionless demeanor. “Jayce, I can't stay here. I'm being called somewhere.”

“Do you even hear yourself right now? You were gone. Literally dead V!! Then you were unconscious for days and I had no clue if you would survive.”

Jayce was getting more worked up the more he spoke. “All I could do was stay by your side and pray to anyone listening that it worked. That you would come back to me. And you did!”

“Have I?,” Viktor asked face almost unreadable again. “Have I truly come back? Jayce I don’t look nor do I feel human.”

“I don’t care how you look. You’re still you! Of course you’re still human,” Jayce tried to reassure both of them. “I’m sure you’ll be back to normal soon!”

“You have played with things beyond your understanding and I don’t know how to fix it. Normal doesn’t exist for me anymore,” Viktor sighed, turning to leave gripping his cane tightly. “I have to go Jayce.”

“Fix it? Viktor there’s nothing to fix, you can’t leave now!,” Jayce pleaded as he grabbed his partners free arm trying to get him to see his point.

Viktor whipped around and glared as he yanked his arm away. “Look at me and tell me I belong here! Why couldn’t you just listen to me for once. I asked you for one thing. One single thing Jayce!”

“Viktor please I couldn’t lose you. I need you. It was the only way to save you!,” Jayce’s voice shook from the strain of his emotions.

“Save me,” Viktor repeated incredulously. “Jayce, you have condemned me! I am a monstrosity. A new weapon to add to the ones you’ve created behind my back!”

“Oh this again? Viktor I didn’t have a choice!,” Like usual their emotions seemed to be building on one another's.

“It seems you never do,” Viktor scoffed.

“You want to talk about doing things behind each other's backs? Fine! Let’s talk about how before the attack you somehow gained some new scars that looked suspiciously like runes!,” Jayce gestured violently. “What the hell were you thinking!”

Viktor’s face closed off completely. “I wasn’t thinking. I was dying and desperate. That’s all there is to it. But you can’t compare what I did and what you’ve done Jayce.”

“Why can’t I? Viktor I never expected this from you!,” Jayce exclaimed. “Why didn’t you come to me! How could you have done that to yourself!”

“How could I?,” Viktor couldn’t believe the audacity of the man he called his partner. “Are you even hearing yourself right now? What the hell are you saying! None of this even matters right now!”

“Of course it matters! You fucking mutilated yourself Vikor! And I only found out when I was trying to bring you back to life!,” Jayce yelled, his features twisted into something ugly.

Viktor, despite the circumstances and literally everything going on, preferred this version of Jayce to the perfectly crafted mask of a politician he seemed to wear constantly nowadays.

It felt like this was the first time he was getting a glimpse of his partner in months. If not years.

Shame this was a farce of a reunion.

“None of this matters, do you know why? I’m supposed to be dead Jayce! I was dead,” Viktor began to pace to his best ability still feeling numb body wise. “You have meddled with the natural order of things beyond anything we ever did or anything I ever theorized!”

“Yes we’ve both done things we shouldn’t have and made mistakes and yes maybe if we talked we could have stopped each other but Jayce we didn’t. It’s too late.”

Jayce looked like he wanted to cry. It was only now Viktor realized that he was injured. His chest was wrapped in bandages and he was dressed in the pajama pants he got him for his birthday years ago.

They were almost Talis red and were covered in cartoon dogs. From the state of them they were well worn and well loved. He was even wearing the matching tacky slippers he got him.

His partner clearly had been sleeping in the lab near what could only be referred to as his chrysalis. Jayce must have meant what he said about staying by his side literally.

As much as a part of Viktor wanted to soften and go easy on him and give in, it was not the time.

It was too late for that.

Viktor was made of metal and magic now. He had used all his gentleness on a man who he barely recognized anymore.

Honestly Viktor didn’t even recognize himself anymore.

Even without the Arcane whispering and urging him to leave there was nothing to stay here for anymore.

“I guess we've both disappointed each other,” Viktor could already feel himself losing the grip on his emotions. They were becoming lost to him again.

“It’s time for me to go,” Viktor stopped pacing and exhaled. “You have to let me go.”

“Absolutely not! I just got you back!,” Jayce looked like he wanted to grab him again and was barely stopping himself from doing so.

“You don’t need me anymore. You haven’t for a long time now,” it pained Viktor to admit outloud what he had been thinking ever since Jayce became part of the council but it had to be said.

“That’s not true,” Jayce looked like he was going to cry. “You can’t possibly believe that.”

“Our paths have diverged a long time ago,” Viktor continued twisting the knife into the wound of his heart that never healed. “It was affection that held us together.”

“I must leave this place,” Viktor went to his desk and grabbed his book containing his research. He then noticed the diagram of him on the blackboard and Jayce’s frantic notes. How Jayce’s desk was used for the first time in who knows how long.

Bitterness rose up but it was gone before he could fully grasp it. The Arcane’s work no doubt. The one relief he had from this butchered resurrection.

Viktor turned to leave and began to walk away. Leaving the one who held his heart if he even had one anymore behind. “Goodbye Jayce.”

Before he could make it to the door Jayce wrapped his arms around him from behind as he buried his face into his back, freezing him in place as well as making him drop his cane and book.

“What are you doing,” Viktor closed his eyes trying not to scream. “Jayce let me go. I need to pick up my things so I can leave.”

Jayce said nothing, only clung tighter but Viktor could feel the way he burrowed his head against him and the wetness of the other man’s tears.

Viktor couldn’t remember the last time Jayce cried around him.

“Viktor please,” Jayce’s voice was muffled as he spoke against flesh turned metal hidden behind a blanket. “Please, you can’t leave.”

Surely Jayce must have been uncomfortable. There was no give to his body, no softness to be seen. Yet he remained wrapped around Viktor with no intention of letting go.

“I made mistakes. We drifted apart- I drifted apart. I should have been by your side. I never should have left you. I’m sorry,” the words were everything Viktor had wanted to hear when he was on what could have been his deathbed while Jayce was bedding Medarda.

It made something ugly and sharp rise in Viktor’s chest. A mess of emotions that he could only hope the Arcane would get rid of.

“Let me go,” Viktor barely managed to get out.

“No,” Jayces tears started to soak the blanket he had wrapped him in. “I can’t lose you again. Please don’t make me lose you again.”

“Jayce please,” Viktor hated how it sounded like he was begging.

“There’s nothing I wouldn't do for you. I pulled you back from death itself Viktor. I will always want you by my side.”

This was a new kind of hell.

Like he was in a nightmarish version of a younger Viktor’s fantasy. If he had anything in his stomach or if he thought he was capable of it anymore Viktor would have been sick.

“I should have told you before-,”

No.

“I didn’t fully understand but I do now-“,

Please.

“Viktor I love you.”

Anything but this.

“I was planning to tell you. I swear I was. But I was too late. It seems I’m always too late.”

Viktor longed to crawl back into his magically spun coffin and fall back into death's embrace. What had he done to deserve this?

“I never knew you could be this cruel,” Viktor rasped as he felt his eyes begin to burn with the now foreign sensation of tears. “Jayce let me go. Please.”

The arms wrapped around Viktor tightened in response, further pinning him in place. Jayce was always stronger than him and his newly reborn body was no match.

Even after all this time Viktor was still so weak.

Emotionally and physically.

How absolutely pathetic.

“I love you,” Jayce repeated, still crying and still talking despite Viktor desperately wanting him to shut up. “I have from the beginning. I never felt anything like it before and I was confused.”

Viktor could only close his eyes tightly as his own tears fell wishing to be anywhere but here. Where were the Arcane side effects when he needed them?

“I know you asked me to destroy the core and I’m sorry I couldn’t but it was my only chance of saving you. I would do it again if I had to if it meant you’d be here.”

“Viktor it felt like my life only truly began once we met. I swear the moment I saw your body it felt like it ended. I can’t do this without you. I can’t. I simply refuse.”

“You’d manage,” Viktor said, his eyes still closed as if that would make this whole situation go away. “You have before.”

“Not like this I haven’t. Your loss, however brief, had destroyed me. I won’t let this happen again. I won’t lose you again. Not from you dying, leaving or even to yourself. I swear it.”

Jayce’s arms were wrapped tight enough around him that if Viktor were in his old body he’d be in a good amount of pain. Now it was only somewhat uncomfortable.

Jayce shifted around and at the movement and ominous tone of his voice Viktor opened his eyes. Jayce was still holding him in place but now they were face to face.

“I love you,” Jayce said as if he couldn’t get tired of saying it. His bloodshot eyes never leaving Viktor’s face for even a second.

“Viktor I love you,” Jace stressed again before he pressed their foreheads together to get his point across even further.

Viktor felt like he’d been gutted at the affectionate gesture from his culture. One that signified love beyond words.

Oh Janna above.

The Arcane’s barely noticeable whispers finally faded into nothing for now as Jayce’s rambling drowned them out.

“I’ll prove myself to you again. I swear I will. We have time now Viktor. As long as we’re together everything will be okay. I will never let you go and you’ll never be hurt again by me I promise.”

Jayce pulled back and began to press desperate kisses all over his face. Across his forehead, the now half hidden mole under his eye, his nose, his cheekbones and the mole above his mouth. Then he kissed his lips.

Although he’d been awake for almost an hour now it felt like only now was Viktor truly brought back to life. All he could do was let him and kiss back.

Breathless Jayce pulled back hovering over his lips. “Promise me you won’t leave. Please Viktor.”

Maybe Viktor would regret this but everything was a mess and he had always been a creature of want.

“Fine. Fine I’ll stay,” he barely got the words out before Jayce swooped back in and attacked his mouth again.

When they finally stumbled into one of their bedrooms what seemed like hours would then be spent with Jayce proving his love and showing just how glad he was that he was alive.

Successfully satisfying both of their longings.

Jayce meant every word and he would do anything to never lose Viktor again. He would do anything to keep him alive and by side.

No matter what it took.
