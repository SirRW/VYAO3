### Text

The lab was whirling with sounds of tinkering and scribbling of formulas. Viktor was used to it, in a way it was soothing to him - as it would allow him to focus on something other than the thoughts in his head. Though, before he could ease into the noise, someone interrupted him.

The doors of their lab swung open, as Jayce rushed through the doors. He seemed excited about something, if his stupid grin were anything to go by. In his arms, he carried a stack of documents which he laid carefully on the table next to Viktor.

“V, look! Our work was finally accepted by the council!” His voice was deeper than usual, must have just woken up Viktor assumes.

“That’s good.” Was all Viktor could muster before Jayce picked up the documents once more.

He flipped through the pages, as though he was reading it for the first time. Viktor found it charming, how his eyes scanned over the words that they wrote together - the perfect result of their shared efforts finally collected and approved. But he couldn’t find it in himself to smile.

As Jayce closed the documents, he stared at the cover page with love in his eyes. That was when he noticed something. It was obvious to Viktor, with how Jayce’s eyes narrowed and locked into one detail. He was observant, curious, perhaps for his own good.

“Still admiring your own work?” Viktor jokes, with a shake of his head.

But Jayce didn’t buy it, in fact, he looked.. Upset? This time, he slammed the documents on the table. It startled Viktor, he nearly dropped his tools. He turned to look at Jayce, he raised the goggles above his eyes to shoot him an ugly look. But Jayce’s back was turned.

“Jayce, what was the meaning of that?” He raised his voice, he didn’t think his joke was that offensive. Jayce was used to Viktor messing with him, why now throw a fit?

“I’m fine. Are you thirsty? I can grab us some coffee.” He moved away from Viktor’s sight, his body still facing away from him. By the looks of his shoulders, though, Viktor knew Jayce was breathing rather heavily.

He didn’t want to push it so he hummed in agreement. He waits for his partner to come back with a Man Of Progress cup, one that places Jayce’s stupid grin in the front. He did ask if Viktor wanted one and he made a remark which translates to ‘what an idiotic question’. Still, he took it.

“Thank you,” Viktor said to break the silence, it had done little to ease the tension resting in Jayce’s shoulders.

Jayce muttered a “You’re welcome”, before going back to the silent treatment. Viktor’s face contorted, he was annoyed. Jayce, who was honest to a fault, would often mince his words in the face of Viktor. Was he worried about offending him? What nonsense. Viktor would never let something like emotions get the better of him.

“What is wrong with you?” Viktor asked bluntly, his arms gesturing towards the man.

Jayce grumbled something, before he replied more loudly: “It’s nothing, Viktor.”

Viktor scoffed, “Well, it doesn’t sound like it.” He narrowed his eyes, zeroing on Jayce’s body language. It was as though he was dissecting him. Maybe he was, who’s to say.

Jayce sighed, he finally turned to look back at Viktor. He rubbed his lips together, indecisive as to whether or not to use them. When he finally opened his mouth, his first statement was an accusation.

“Your name isn’t on the documents.” Jayce spoke plainly. If Viktor thought about it more, he would’ve thought Jayce was guilty.

Viktor couldn’t bite back his chuckle.

He smiled slightly, “Yes, I am well aware.” He raised an eyebrow, curious.

Meanwhile, Jayce looked like he short-circuited, “Wait, you knew?” He asked, like he was uncovering a big secret.

Viktor hummed, as he pretended to be busy, “I assumed I would, considering I requested it.” He waited for Jayce’s response, he was half expecting anger or even pity for him. But instead, he received silence. It was deafening indeed. He tried to not let it bother him.

Finally, Jayce spoke softly, “Okay.” And that was that.

Weeks passed since that conversation, Viktor thought nothing of it - maybe a bit odd that Jayce cared so much but he didn’t question it. That was until he came across Mel Medarda while roaming the halls of the Academy. She gave him a polite but curt smile as she approached him. He froze out of sheer confusion, he scrunched up his nose.

“Congratulations on your partnership.” Her voice was unwavering, but carried some emotion Viktor couldn’t discern.

He tilted his head, “Uh, thank you?”

She nodded again, “Though, I assumed it was a private affair, considering Jayce hardly speaks on it.”

He blinked, once then twice. What is she talking about? It was known for years that they have been working together.

“Still, I would like to give you a proper wedding gift.”

That’s when Viktor sputtered, “Wedding? What are you talking about? Who got married?”

Now it was her turn to appear confused. She scoffed, a slight smile on her face, “You and Jayce? Or am I mistaken?” Her eyes twinkled with amusement.

You are, you are definitely mistaken. Viktor wants to shout, to scream, but his mouth could not move. Instead, he said, “You are right, we- we are partners.” He decided, with determination, then he held his hand out for the gift.

She nodded and handed him a small box.

Viktor ran a thumb over the wooden case, then he placed it into his pocket, “I will share this gift with Jayce, thank you Miss Medarda.” He nodded, his body still stiff.

He continued to walk, brushing past Mel. As the sound of Viktor’s cane against the tile floor filled the quiet air, he could’ve sworn he heard Mel chuckle as he turned the corner. He fiddled with his keys, as he unlocked the lab.

When he entered the lab, he welcomed the sight of Jayce over his desk. Gently, he placed the box onto the table beside Jayce then gingerly peeked over Jayce’s shoulder.

“Hey V,” Jayce talked with a smirk, as if he was acting smug about identifying Viktor’s watchful eyes. It wasn’t that impressive of a guess, considering how much time he spent in the lab anyways.

“Jayce,” he remarked plainly as he ran his fingers across the table, “why did Mel ask if we were married?”

Jayce choked on his words, he ducked his head, and looked away. Viktor’s gaze never left his side, still observing him like prey. Although his words were clear, there was an underlying message: what did you do?

Jayce took a moment to collect his thoughts until he finally admitted what happened, “Probably because I wrote Viktor and Jayce Talis on the notes.”

Viktor’s mouth was agape, his eyebrows shot up. The air became thick, as the room grew silent - minus the whirring of the gears. Both men shared a look, they knew each so well that they could read each other like a book. Viktor’s eyes were scolding the taller, while Jayce’s puppy dog eyes tried to apologize.

“Why would you do that? Without my permission, no less?” Viktor’s voice did not raise, however Jayce could feel the heat radiating off from it. His words are like scorching lava.

“You told me, you wanted to be remembered.” Jayce extended his arms, “This is that something.”

Viktor’s breath was heavy, words thicke on his tongue.

Viktor wanted to cut down Jayce’s words but instead, he looked away, “You should’ve told me.” He held onto his cane tightly, as he worried his lip.

Jayce reached out to Viktor, his fingers brushing against Viktor’s forearms, “I’m sorry Viktor, I didn’t mean to push anything onto you.”

Viktor sighed, he shook his head: “I should tell you why.” He pulled out a chair and sat down. He laid his cane over his lap and held onto it like a lifeline. He tapped his fingers against the metal, “Since I came to the city, I always felt like I was intruding on the people. They looked at me with disgust, or even pity. I don’t wish for either, but you - you do not look at me with those same looks. I am not ashamed of our work, nor do I wish for it to be buried. But, I also know that the people would not accept me as your partner - they need a symbol. It’s tragic, but-”

Before Viktor could say anymore, Jayce instinctively held onto Viktor’s hands.

“Viktor.. You are my partner, no matter what or where you came from.” Jayce’s grasp tightened, “Your work… our work would never be accomplished without you, I need you too. I will not allow your name to be unnoticed because of what some pompous people think.” He kissed the inside of Viktor’s hands, “You’re perfect.”

Viktor looked at him, his eyes wide before he stood up and planted a kiss on Jayce’s lips. Jayce returned the kiss, with similar force. Jayce’s hand found their way into Viktor’s hair, combing through his locks, while Viktor’s were against Jayce’s chest.

As the two kissed, they broke away to collect themselves.

“I love you.” Viktor spoke as easily as he could breathe.

“I love you too,” Jayce smiled, “partner.”

They did not have to say a word to know how they felt about the other, but they still needed to hear the words sometimes. The word partner meant everything to them, it was like a love confession without the three words, now with the added phrase it meant something else: something deeper and more complex.

Their love would transcend all bonds, Viktor determined.

They hugged, Jayce’s arms wrapped tightly around Viktor’s waist. Viktor’s played with the tufts of Jayce’s hair, as he hummed to himself in contentment.

For what felt like years, Jayce began to lift his hands, “Now, I want to know what gift Mel gave us.” Jayce smiled as he pulled away from his partner’s embrace.

Viktor missed the contact but he could not deny he was interested too. With a click, Jayce opens the box and reveals what was inside. It was a jewelry box, lined with silk. Inside was a golden chain necklace, hanging off the chain was a blue crystal - reminiscent of the hextech the two men working on. An engraving on the back near the clasp - V + J.
